Band Overview

Smoking Snakes are a Gothenburg-based Swedish sleaze metal and hard rock band combining the attitude and swagger of '80s glam metal with the power, hooks and energy of modern hard rock.

Formed in 2022, the band have quickly established an international profile through their debut album Danger Zone, released worldwide through Frontiers Music Srl in 2024, and their follow-up album All Lights On, released in 2026.

Drawing inspiration from the golden era of hard rock and sleaze metal, including W.A.S.P., RATT, Dokken and KISS, Smoking Snakes deliver a sound built around infectious choruses, powerful vocals, razor-sharp guitar riffs and soaring melodies.

With two albums released through Frontiers Music Srl, international touring experience, Rocknytt Prize recognition and a reputation for delivering energetic live performances, Smoking Snakes continue to build their profile on stage and on record.

Biography

Emerging from Gothenburg, Sweden, Smoking Snakes were formed in 2022 with a clear mission: to bring the attitude, energy and larger-than-life spirit of classic '80s hard rock and sleaze metal into the modern era.

Featuring Brett Martin on lead vocals and rhythm guitar, Andy Delarge on bass and backing vocals, Mackey Gee on drums and backing vocals, and Rick Styles on lead guitar and backing vocals, the band quickly developed a sound that combines the melodic strengths of classic hard rock with the raw edge and swagger of Scandinavian sleaze metal.

Their influences include W.A.S.P., RATT, Dokken and KISS, but Smoking Snakes bring their own identity to the genre through memorable songwriting, infectious hooks, powerful guitar work and an unmistakable sense of rock 'n' roll attitude.

Even before the release of their debut album, the band were attracting attention. With only a handful of singles available, Smoking Snakes became the most-voted act to perform at the iconic Time To Rock festival.

Their debut full-length album, Danger Zone, arrived in 2024 through international rock and metal label Frontiers Music Srl. The album received an impressive response from the international rock press. Metal Temple awarded the record a perfect 10/10, describing it as a sleaze metal masterpiece, while Metal Forces Magazine awarded it 8/10 and praised its driving heavy rock, swagger and infectious energy. Metal-Roos also recognised the album's quality, describing it as a breathtaking debut with an Album of the Year aura.

Further recognition followed when Danger Zone became a finalist in two categories at the Rocknytt Prize: Album of the Year and Breakthrough Artist of the Year.

In 2026, Smoking Snakes returned with their second album, All Lights On, again released through Frontiers Music Srl. The new record represents the natural evolution of the band's songwriting, expanding their musical identity while retaining the classic hard rock foundations, sleaze metal attitude and arena-ready choruses that first brought them attention.

Alongside their growing catalogue, Smoking Snakes have continued developing their international live presence, performing across Sweden and Europe, including UK festival appearances and international touring dates.
